FAQ

Questions families ask us

Where is my family's data stored?
In Canada. Records are encrypted on your device before they're uploaded, so our servers only ever hold encrypted data, and only the devices you authorize can read it. It's stored in a Canadian data region on infrastructure certified to SOC 2 Type 2. We never sell your data.
Who can see my records?
Only the people you invite, and only what you choose to share. Each person controls their own records, so a parent can keep anything private, even from family.
Does my parent need to be good with technology?
No. The senior view uses large text, big buttons and only a few screens. Family members can also help add records on their behalf.
Does it connect to MyChart or BC Health Gateway?
Not yet. For now, you can add a record from any source by snapping a photo or uploading a PDF, which works with every clinic and province. Connecting to provincial portals is on our roadmap.
How much does it cost?
There's a free plan to get started. The Family plan is $8 CAD per month, or $80 per year, for your whole care circle. You can try the live demo without an account or credit card.
Can I use it today?
Vitalitree is in early release. You can try the full experience in our live demo now.
