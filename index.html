The problem

Family health information is everywhere except where you need it.

Scattered

Easy to miss

Refills, follow-ups and what the doctor actually said are forgotten by the drive home.

FAQ

Questions families ask us

Still have a question?

We're happy to help. Email us at hello@vitalitree.ca*

Where is my family's data stored?
Your records are encrypted and stored on servers in Canada.* We never sell your data.*
Who can see my records?
Only the people you invite, and only what you choose to share. Each person controls their own records, so a parent can keep anything private, even from family.
Does my parent need to be good with technology?
No. The senior view uses large text, big buttons and only a few screens. Family members can also help add records on their behalf.
Does it connect to MyChart or BC Health Gateway?
Not directly today. Instead, you can add a record from any source by snapping a photo or uploading a PDF, which works with every clinic and province. *
How much does it cost?
The Family plan is $8 CAD per month for your whole care circle.* You can try the live demo for free without an account or credit card.
Can I use it today?
Vitalitree is in early release. You can try the full experience in our live demo now, and join the waitlist for launch.*
